### who is she

Erika doesn't exist. She was built from scratch with three AI tools and a set of rules that keep her face the same in every clip.

- 5 days live
- 13 videos
- 26.6K followers, 143K likes, top clip at 1M views
- $10K from ad deals on those videos

### the stack

| step | tool | job |
|---|---|---|
| look | ChatGPT | face, outfit, consistent character sheet |
| mind | Claude | personality, scenes, video prompts |
| motion | Higgsfield | turns the stills into video |

### the idea

People don't need to relearn who Erika is every time. The face stays fixed, the situation changes. Shaolin temple, mountain ridge, parkour roof, gym mirror. Same girl, new world.
